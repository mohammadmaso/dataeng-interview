## In plain words (Junior)
Instead of a messy pile of files, you ship a well-labeled, reliable dataset with a named owner you can call if something is wrong.

## How it works (Mid)
A data product bundles data, metadata, schema, access, SLAs and a contract, discoverable in a catalog. Owners treat consumers as customers: versioning, deprecation notices, support and quality metrics. Central to data mesh thinking.

## Senior angle & interview tips
The hard part is organizational: ownership, funding and incentives. Tooling includes data contracts, catalogs, tests and usage metrics. Measure adoption and trust, not just table counts.

Interview question: "What distinguishes a data product from a table?" Answer: explicit ownership, contract, SLA, documentation, and lifecycle management for its consumers.
